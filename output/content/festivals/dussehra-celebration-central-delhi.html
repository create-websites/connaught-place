🏛️ Spatial Configuration & Venue Matrix

Connaught Place serves as a commercial buffer between Old and New Delhi. While CP's Central Park hosts peaceful civic displays, major pyrotechnics and effigy immolations (Ravana Dahan) are strategically shifted to open spaces within a 1–3 km radius.

🛡️ Security Protocols & Traffic Diversion Hub

Multi-tier counter-terror, fire safety cordons, aerial drone monitoring, and daily 5 PM to Midnight traffic diversions safeguard hundreds of thousands of celebrants.

🚧 Active Traffic Diversion Points (17:00 – Midnight)

Netaji Subhash Marg Corridor

Restricted from Delhi Gate to Chhatta Rail Chowk. DTC Buses & heavy vehicles diverted via Ring Road.

JLN Marg & Asaf Ali Road Axis

Rolling diversions during VVIP movements near Ramlila Maidan. Radial diversions at Daryaganj Chowk.

Connaught Place Radial Arterials

Barakhamba Rd, Janpath & KG Marg diverted near outer circle during peak evening crowd dispersal.

🛑 Security Guidelines & Prohibited Items

All visitors pass through Door Frame Metal Detectors (DFMDs) and physical frisking with Handheld Metal Detectors (HHMDs). Zero-tolerance policy enforced for:

✕ Large Backpacks / Luggage
✕ Personal Fireworks / Explosives
✕ Power Banks / Battery Packs
✕ Sharp Objects & Combustibles
Fire Safety Cordon: Double-ringed steel perimeter barricades keep crowds at mandatory safe radii from effigy ignition zones, supported by on-site high-pressure Delhi Fire Service tenders.

🌱 Modernization & Sustainability Outlook

Balancing centuries-old cultural performance with contemporary air quality standards, environmental regulations, and intelligent urban security management.

🎆 Green Pyrotechnics

Transition toward low-emission chemical formulations reduces particulate emissions during Ravana Dahan without altering traditional visual impact.

💡 Digital Laser & Projections

Venues like SBKK and Central Park leverage high-definition digital projections, multilingual subtitles, and laser displays to substitute heavy combustion.

🚁 AI & Drone Surveillance

Real-time aerial crowd density analysis feeds directly into unified command vehicles to dynamically control gate entries before critical thresholds are breached.
