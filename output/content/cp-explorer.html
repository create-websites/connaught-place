Time Capsule

A Century of Evolution

Designed by Robert Tor Russell and named after the Duke of Connaught, this architectural marvel took 4 years to build.

1

1929 - The Beginning

Construction begins. The goal: create a commercial hub for Lutyens' Delhi to rival the finest in Europe.

1933 - Grand Opening

Completed and named after Prince Arthur, 1st Duke of Connaught. It becomes the elite shopping destination for the Raj.

2
3

1995-2005 - The Metro Era

The arrival of Delhi Metro (Rajiv Chowk) transforms connectivity. Renamed formally to Rajiv Chowk, though 'CP' sticks.

Present Day

A mix of high-street fashion, bustling cafes, and historical charm. The 9th most expensive office market in the world.

4

Georgian Style, Radial Logic

Modeled after the Royal Crescent in Bath, Connaught Place is structured as two concentric circles creating three distinct zones.

  • 1 Inner Circle (Blocks A-F): The premium retail hub. Iconic columns and Palladian arches.
  • 2 Middle Circle: The connecting road housing banks, offices, and smaller eateries.
  • 3 Outer Circle (Blocks G-P): A mix of fine dining, newer cafes, and the famous K-block nightlife.

🏛️ Insight: There are 7 radial roads leading out from the center, dividing the blocks. This makes navigation tricky for newcomers always know your Block letter!
