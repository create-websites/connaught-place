Rajghat


 

Rajghat is the final resting place of Mahatma Gandhi, Father of the Nation. The cremation of Mahatma Gandhi was held at Raj Ghat Delhi only, January 31, 1950. The memorial of Mahatma Gandhi placed there is a simple square platform of black stone with the words "Hey Ram" inscribed near it. As a tribute to the Mahatma, an eternal flame in a copper urn, it continues to burn. Around the monument is a low wall, simple, no frills. Every Friday, a memorial ceremony held at the Delhi Raj Ghat National Memorial.
