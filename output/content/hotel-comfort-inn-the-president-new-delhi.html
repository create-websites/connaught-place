Hotel Comfort Inn The President, New Delhi

 

Comfort Inn President is located close to Connaught Place, which is a commercial and trading hub and a city centre location in New Delhi.

 

Accommodation : In-room facilities include a mini bar, LCD TV, tea and coffee maker Wi-Fi internet connectivity and electronic safes.

 

Restaurants / Cafes: “Café 23” a Multi-cuisine Resto Bar offers wide variety of choicest Continental cuisines, Indian Tandori dishes, Curry and Kebabs.

 

Location

Centrally located in New Delhi's city center

10 minutes drive from the hub of New Delhi's embassy row

Close to - the President’s Estate, Parliament House, Pragati Maidan, all the State Emporiums and New Delhi Railway Station

15 minutes drive from some of New Delhi's favorite monuments, the national zoo, and the national museum

 

Distances

 

Domestic Airport - 11 km

Railway Station - 1 km

Business District - 1 km

Connaught Place - 0.5 km

International Trade Fair Ground(Pragati Maidan) - 4 km

Government Offices and Ministries - 2 km

Diplomatic Enclave - 3 km

International Airport - 15 km
